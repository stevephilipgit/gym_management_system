❓ FAQ & COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Will this require downtime?
A: NO. All changes are backward-compatible. Deploy during low traffic for safety.

Q: Can I rollback if something goes wrong?
A: YES. Simply revert endpoint URL in frontend from /summary/today to /today.
   Old endpoint still works as fallback.

Q: How long will implementation take?
A: 90 minutes for full deployment. Could be 30 minutes for basic summary-only.

Q: Do I need to restart everything?
A: YES, restart backend after changes. Frontend refresh recommended.

Q: Will my existing data be affected?
A: NO. Implementation doesn't modify existing collections, only adds new one.

Q: How do I verify it's working?
A: Follow the verification steps in QUICK_START_GUIDE.txt Step 5.

Q: What if there's an error?
A: See TROUBLESHOOTING section in IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.txt.

Q: Can I implement this partially?
A: YES. Summary table works independently. Pagination is optional.

Q: Will this affect existing API users?
A: NO. All changes are additions. Existing endpoints unchanged.

Q: How often do I need to maintain this?
A: Monitoring is automatic. Daily midnight reset is automatic.

Q: What happens at midnight?
A: Yesterday's summary is locked, new summary created for today.

Q: Can I check system health?
A: YES. Use /api/finance/diagnostics endpoint to get full status.
